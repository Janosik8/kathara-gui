# The device pictures

`router.jpg`, `switch.jpg`, `pc.jpg` and `server.jpg` are from Cisco's network topology
icons, https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/about/brand-center/network-topology-icons.html

Cisco: "Use of these element icons (in an unmodified format) is authorized, without
additional permission from Cisco." (https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/about/brand-center/copyright-use.html, read on 2026-11-01.)

They are not covered by this repository's MIT licence. The PNGs the canvas draws,
`frontend/src/canvas/icons/`, are the same pictures with the white ground made transparent
by `scripts/icons_to_png.py`: the shapes and colours are Cisco's, unchanged.
